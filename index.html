THE GAME As a Medici banker you will travel through France, Spain, the Holy Roman Empire, and Rome to collect money to support Giovanni de' Medici as Pope. Your rivals see how much Influence you have, and you see theirs — but you cannot see your own. Gather Cardinals in the cities where you find Support. When enough Cardinals have gathered, call a Conclave — and become the Pope's Depositary General of the Papal Treasury! YOUR TURN — 3 MOVES Click a Banker, then a lit step: 1 move per step. Reach a road's end to complete it. Any time on your turn: Influence 3ƒ (one, faces away) · Support 3ƒ (any number, secret — Rome pays 1ƒ per card) · Bond 3ƒ (two at a time). A COMPLETION, IN ORDER 1 cash any bonds · 2 collect the pay · 3 seat a Cardinal (optional, paid) · 4 take the favor — or at Rome, Call the Conclave. THE CONCLAVE Quorum is 5 / 6 / 7 by player count; two past quorum forces the doors. Schemes may strike before the ballots are read. THE MEDICI BANK 3ƒ in · matures 4 → 5 → 6 at the start of your turns · cash at any completion. All bonds are public.
